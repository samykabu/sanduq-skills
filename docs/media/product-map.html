Six skills in three bundles

illustration-tools holds illustrate; dev-tools holds user-manual and github-wiki-publish; agent-tools holds delegate-task, progress-report and planning-majlis. user-manual draws with illustrate when it is installed, and planning-majlis can run its critics through delegate-task. Every skill also works on its own. The samykabu/sanduq repository lists the three bundles in its plugin marketplace and copies illustrate, user-manual, delegate-task and progress-report into its Spec Kit extensions.
